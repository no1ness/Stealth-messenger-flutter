Signature elements

The "what makes Stealth unmistakably Stealth" pieces. Each auto-disables in Brightness.light — light mode is the accessibility / high-contrast face of the app and deliberately skips signature ornaments. Each panel previews the effect at full intensity over the same sample subtree.

ScanlineOverlay

effects/scanline_overlay.dart
Thin horizontal stripes at scanlineSpacingPx: 4, scanlineOpacity: 0.06. Wired on outgoing chat bubbles (intensity 0.5), DialogImportance.high bodies, and the in-call E2E ENCRYPTED badge.

GrainOverlay

effects/grain_overlay.dart
Procedural noise at grainOpacity: 0.04. Felt as "the screen has dust on it" — not noticed. Used on registration background and behind StealthEmptyState surfaces.

ChromaticAberration

effects/chromatic_aberration.dart
FOCUS
RGB-split lens-focus cue. Renders the subtree once as the interactive baseline plus two ghosts (red shifted left, cyan shifted right) by aberrationDxPx: 1.5. Wired into GlassTextField as a 1 → 0 pulse on focus gain.

DecryptText

motion/decrypt_text.dart
Character-cipher reveal. Each char starts as a random hex digit and resolves to its target value at a staggered point along AppMotion.slow (400 ms). Wired on the loading-screen STEALTH wordmark — the first thing every user sees on cold launch.
