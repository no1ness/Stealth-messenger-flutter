In-call HUD

The hardest moment to get right and the easiest to recognise. Three things carry the signature:

Mono duration + safety-number groups + the scan-line-clad E2E ENCRYPTED badge.
  • Connection chip top-left in GeistMono caps with a 6px glowing dot — reads as telemetry, not as decoration.
  • Avatar halo with a slow 24-second rotating dashed ring in system-blue. Subliminal "we're still connected".
  • Safety-number groups directly under the name. Hex pairs in GeistMono = "this is a value out of openssl, not a settings field".
  • Mono duration at 56/1 GeistMono with 6px letter-spacing. Glow drops the size from "a number" to "a clock you can stare at".
  • E2E ENCRYPTED badge. The one thing a user will remember Stealth for. Blue glow + scan-line + 2.4s pulse.
  • Telemetry strip at the bottom-third — transport / codec / latency. Cyberpunk-HUD energy, not iOS-stock.
  • Hangup button is the only red surface on screen. Visual gravity does the work.
